3. Use mock mode: set EXPO_PUBLIC_USE_MOCK=true in your .env (do not commit .env).
4. Mock conversations and dispute responses are provided in mockChats.ts and ordersService.ts.

RULES FOR YOU AND YOUR AI AGENT (these prevent merge conflicts)
1. Edit ONLY the files listed as yours. If you need to change a file you do not own, STOP and message me first.
2. Do NOT edit shared/types.ts, shared/components, theme, ProfileScreen.tsx or OrderDetailScreen.tsx in
